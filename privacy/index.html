Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short

Cambiora needs no account and contains no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools. We do not collect any personal data. What you set up in the app stays on your device.

Controller

René Winkler
Email: contact@cambiora.com

Data on your device

Cambiora stores locally on your device:

  • your settings (e.g. theme, language, number format),
  • your selected currencies, their order and the last amount you entered,
  • the most recently loaded exchange rates and rate history, so the app works offline,
  • the settings of your home screen widgets,
  • your rate alerts (currency pair, target rate and when it was reached); they are checked on your device only and trigger local notifications.

This data does not leave your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the system settings. Whether it is included in your device backup (e.g. Google or iCloud backup) depends on your system settings.

If you want, Cambiora creates a backup file with your currency lists, custom rates, rate alerts and settings under Settings → Backup. The file is only created when you start it and is saved where you choose in the save dialog (e.g. your Downloads folder or your cloud storage). Cambiora does not send it to us or to anyone else. When importing, the app only reads the file you select yourself.

Loading exchange rates

To load current rates, the app requests public rate services directly:

  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) for the European Central Bank reference rates and rate history,
  • currency-api via jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) or Cloudflare Pages (currency-api.pages.dev) for other currencies and precious metals,
  • Coinbase (api.coinbase.com) for cryptocurrencies.

These requests contain no information about you, your selection or your amounts. As with any internet request, the providers technically receive your IP address, the time and information about the device type or app version. The respective providers are responsible for this processing under their own privacy policies. We do not receive this data.

The home screen widgets also refresh rates through these services, about once per hour in the background, as long as a widget is placed or a rate alert is active.

Purchases (Cambiora Pro)

Buying Cambiora Pro is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive any payment data. The app only learns from the store whether Pro was purchased and stores this status locally.

Camera (price scan)

When you use "Scan prices", the app asks for access to the camera. Instead of the camera you can also pick a photo from your gallery; the app then only receives that one photo. Text recognition runs on your device. Cambiora does not store or transmit the images.

For text recognition the app uses Google ML Kit. ML Kit sends technical usage and diagnostic data to Google, such as the device model, operating system version, app version, a random per-installation identifier, performance metrics and error codes. Images and recognized text are not transmitted. The data is encrypted in transit; Google is responsible for it (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). We do not receive this data.

Local currency

So that Cambiora can suggest the currency of the country you are in when you travel, the app reads the country of the mobile network (Android only) and the device time zone on your device. No location permission is needed, and this information does not leave your device. The app only stores for which countries the suggestion has already been shown.

Error reports

If an unexpected error occurs in the app, Cambiora stores an error report on your device only: time, type of error, a shortened error message, the technical sequence in the program (stack trace), app version, operating system and its version, and the app language. It does not contain amounts, your currency selection, rates or any identifier of your device. Identical errors are grouped, at most 20 reports are kept, and reports older than 90 days are deleted. You can find them under Settings → About → Error reports, where you can view, copy or delete them.

Cambiora never sends the reports by itself. Only if you choose "Send by email" does your email app open with the reports; you see the text before sending and decide yourself whether to send it. We treat an email sent this way like any other contact request: we use it only to fix the error and delete it when it is no longer needed for that. In rare cases an error message may contain part of an input, so please check the text before sending.

Your rights

As we do not collect or store personal data about you, we generally cannot provide information about or delete such data; all app data is stored only on your device and can be deleted there. If you have questions, contact us at contact@cambiora.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Switzerland the FDPIC, in the EU the authority of your country of residence).

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy. The current version is available at https://cambiora.com/privacy/.
