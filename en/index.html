Features

A currency converter that simply converts.

No ads – even for free

No banners, no videos, no pop-ups. Not even in the free version.

Works offline

Rates are stored on your device – on a plane, without roaming or with no signal. You always see how current they are.

Calculate in the amount

Add, subtract, multiply, divide and percent right in the amount. For tips, discounts or a split bill.

More than 150 currencies

Several currencies at once, each with its flag. Search by name, code or country, drag to reorder.

Crypto & precious metals

Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Dogecoin and Litecoin with live prices. Gold, silver and platinum per troy ounce, gram or kilogram.

Rate history

Up to three months free, up to five years with Pro – with high, low and average, for crypto and precious metals too.

Scan prices Pro

Point the camera at a menu or price tag: Cambiora recognizes the prices right on your device, converts them and adds them up.

Home screen widgets Pro

Conversion or rate with trend right on your home screen, each widget with its own currencies. Android for now.

Just the way you like it

Light or dark, your region’s number format like 1,234.50 and as many decimal places as you want. Five color themes with Pro.

Calculate where you type.

No switching to a calculator: the expression goes right into the amount, and all currencies update instantly – exact, without rounding errors.
